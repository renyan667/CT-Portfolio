Arduino Project

Reflection

This is my third computing project ever! This topic was all about Arduinos and Mechatronics. First, I learnt the basics of Arduino IDE by watching some very informative videos to code a basic buzzer on the Arduino. Then, I coded a working F1 style reaction time test on the Arduino that could send results back to my laptop. Here, I had a lot of fun competing with my friends to see who had the best reaction time. After this, I watched a few videos and got to learn all about algorithms, sequencing and looping. From this, I was able to sketch out a flowchart in pseudocode for an automated machine to do the dishes. From here, I brainstormed and sketched out a few ideas for this project and eventually landed on a clock with a motivational quote. My idea was to keep it simple and minimalist for a clean look and easy readability. I kept the font simple and had a black and white colour palette. However, I still encountered a few problems throughout and had to troubleshoot the font size and placement of the date row and the quote. In the end, with a little help from AI, I was able to fix these problems. From this, I learnt to persevere and keep working on my code even when it wasn’t working. One thing I regret not having enough time to do was a weather system pulling from a web API, as I had a perfect space near the top right corner where it would have fit perfectly. My goals for the future are to code more interactive and visually appealling displays and being able to manage my time better. Still, I’m very proud of my design and my ability to overcome the many obstacles that I’ve faced throughout this task and I’m looking forward to doing the next computing tech tasks.
